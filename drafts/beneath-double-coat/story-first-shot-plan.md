## Production responsibilities

- Establish the four speaking characters and their voices before rendering independent setups; reuse those assets across cuts. The stranger shares Bruno’s face reference with a distinct collar.
- Keep hallway geography consistent: breakfast exit, memorial console, grooming door. The audience must see characters travel rather than teleport between unrelated rooms.
- Preserve the matched compositions in the final exterior/interior pair. That comparison is the reveal.
- Render the final three-second title editorially. Composite the thermostat values onto its insert; exact evidence must not depend on generated lettering.
- Continue the grooming performance through s10–s15. This run is 31 seconds, within the existing 40-second continuation limit.
- Use s16–s17 as motivated parallel action: Otto reaches the door while Bruno and Sadie leave breakfast. Carry Heidi’s scratching/shout across both views.
- Never play two speaking turns simultaneously. Allow reactions after “My brother” and “The one you said was dead.”
