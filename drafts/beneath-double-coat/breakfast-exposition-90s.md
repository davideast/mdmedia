# BENEATH THE DOUBLE COAT
## Episode One — The Secret in the Snow
### Opening scene: “Breakfast at Alpenrose”

**Production screenplay · 90 seconds · one continuous morning**

The audience knows nobody. This opening establishes the estate, five members of its household, their relationships, and a small marital tension. The mystery begins in a later scene.

## Cast and performance lock

Every character is a photorealistic Bernese Mountain Dog with a heavy, natural quadrupedal body, floppy ears, and a tricolor double coat. They speak with restrained canine mouth articulation. They remain dogs: objects move with noses, paws, and mouths; nobody acquires human hands, stands like a person, or gestures like an actor in a dog suit.

| ID | Character and relationship | Visual identity | Voice and behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HEIDI | Owner of Alpenrose; Bruno’s grandmother; Penny’s great-grandmother | Elderly female; silver muzzle; narrow white blaze; burgundy collar | Low, measured, imperious older female voice. Expects obedience. Affection lives beneath her authority. |
| OTTO | Estate groundskeeper; trusted, familiar household staff | Stocky adult male; shaggy cheeks; ochre collar | **No dialogue in this opening.** Earnest physical reactions. His eventual voice is a warm adult male voice, never Heidi’s. |
| BRUNO | Heidi’s grandson; Sadie’s husband; Penny’s father, as the household understands it | Largest adult; broad white chest; symmetrical rust eyebrows; navy collar | Warm baritone, casually confident until questioned. Lumbering physical comedy. |
| SADIE | Bruno’s wife; Penny’s mother | Adult female; slimmer build; slender white blaze; emerald collar | Precise adult female voice, dry and controlled; distinct from Heidi’s lower, older voice. |
| PENNY | Bruno and Sadie’s young daughter | Clearly smaller puppy; broad white forehead patch; soft lavender collar | Bright young female voice. Direct and unembarrassed, without exaggerated baby talk. |

The household Bruno is called **Bruno** throughout this scene. No identity-twist terminology enters generation prompts. No additional dogs, humans, narrators, or background voices appear.

Use approved individual character images and the same voice references wherever the production system supports them. A written voice description is direction, not a technical guarantee of a locked voice. If audio must be made separately, retain these speaker IDs through casting, rendering, and lip synchronization.

## Setting and geography lock

One snowbound Alpine estate at breakfast, shortly after dawn. Old money, warm wood, restrained luxury. Soft amber interior light contrasts with blue-white snow outside. No time or weather changes.

The breakfast room is rectangular:

- **West wall / screen left in the master:** one inward-opening casement window, already open. Heidi rests on a low upholstered bench immediately beside it.
- **North wall / rear of the master:** an open doorway into the entrance hall. Otto and Bruno enter here. The exterior front door connects to this hall, a short distance from the window.
- **Center:** a low breakfast table. A small plate holding one cheddar cube sits at the near edge, reachable by Penny.
- **Near-right table corner:** Bruno’s oversized chair. His front half can perch on it while his rear paws remain on the floor.
- **Far side of the table:** Sadie, facing Bruno’s position. Penny starts on the floor beside her and crosses beneath the table toward Bruno.

The camera enters through the west window and settles into a master that shows the window at left, doorway behind the table, and Bruno’s chair at right. Subsequent angles stay on the same side of the Sadie–Bruno conversational axis. Heidi’s look toward Otto and Sadie’s look toward Bruno must match their positions.

**Prop continuity:** the window starts open, closes once, then reopens and stays open. Penny’s bowl starts beside Bruno’s chair, where he displaces it; Sadie retrieves it. The cheddar cube remains on its plate until Penny takes it in the final shot. Otto leaves his snow brush outside before entering.

## Editorial and generation rules

- **Continuation:** extend the actual preceding footage and its final action state. Do not create a fresh text-only interpretation of the next segment.
- **Cut:** a deliberate new camera angle with matching character and room references, current prop state, and the stated action or sound bridge. Hard cut; no fade or dissolve.
- **Offscreen speech:** the named speaker is outside the frame. Visible listeners never mouth that speaker’s words.
- **Listening:** silent, with mouths resting naturally closed. Avoid panting during dialogue if it could read as another character talking.
- Only quoted dialogue below is spoken. Names before dialogue, timing, stage directions, and headings are never spoken. A name within a quoted line is an address, not a speaker switch.
- All time ranges are production targets, not a requirement to speed up speech. Preserve the pause before each comic reaction; adjust silent holds within the shot before compressing delivery.
- Maintain one continuous room ambience across cuts. Music is a sparse original piano motif, quiet beneath speech. No musical restart at segment boundaries.
- Do not regenerate earlier dialogue when extending footage. Each continuation performs only its new action and lines.

## 01 · The estate wakes
### 00:00–00:08 · EXT. ALPENROSE ESTATE — DAWN

**On camera:** Otto only. No other dog visible through the window yet.

**Picture:** Begin at ground-floor height, with enough width to see the snowbound mansion, its steps, and the open window beside the entrance. Move smoothly toward that window. Otto pushes a wide snow brush across the steps with his muzzle. He steps backward across his clean patch and leaves fresh muddy paw prints. He notices. A resigned pause, then he resumes.

**Dialogue:** None. Otto is silent throughout.

**Sound:** Cold wind; bristles scraping; soft collar jingle; faint crockery inside. The first sparse piano notes.

**End state:** Still moving toward the open window, not arriving at a static final composition. Otto is beside the steps; the brush remains outside. The next segment extends this exact trajectory.

## 02 · A voice from inside
### 00:08–00:16 · EXT. TO INT. — CONTINUOUS

**On camera:** Otto outside initially. Heidi becomes visible inside only as the camera passes through the window. Sadie and Penny may become visible at the table near the end. Bruno is absent.

**00:08–00:09:** Continue the approach at the same speed and height.

**00:09–00:11 — HEIDI, OFFSCREEN FROM INSIDE:**
> “Otto! Breakfast.”

Heidi’s low, older female voice comes through the open window. **Otto remains silent, with his mouth closed.** He raises his head and looks toward the voice. The word “Otto” does not transfer the speaking role to him.

**00:11–00:16:** Otto leaves the brush and moves toward the nearby front entrance, exiting the camera’s view. The camera passes through the already-open casement without touching glass or wood. Reveal Heidi beside the window as the source of the call. She is now silent.

**Sound:** Continuous wind gradually gives way to interior room tone. Otto’s footsteps travel toward the entrance rather than following the camera through the window.

**End state:** Camera just inside, still moving gently toward the master position. Heidi at left; Sadie and Penny by the table. Otto has entered the hall offscreen.

## 03 · The household rules
### 00:16–00:24 · INT. BREAKFAST ROOM — CONTINUOUS

**On camera:** Heidi beside the window; Sadie beyond the table; Penny nearby on the floor; Otto enters through the rear doorway. Bruno is absent.

**Picture:** Continue the move and settle into the room master. Otto comes through the established doorway, crosses to the window, and noses its open leaf shut. Sadie quietly attends to breakfast; Penny noses around the table. Neither draws focus from Otto’s action.

**Dialogue:** None. All four dogs are silent.

**Sound:** Otto’s paws on the floor, window hinge, then a latch click at approximately 00:23.5. The wind becomes distinctly muffled.

**End state:** Window closed. Otto beside the latch. Heidi turns her eyes toward him. Cut on that reaction, not before the audience sees what he did.

## 04 · Heidi gets her way
### 00:24–00:31 · CUT TO HEIDI AND OTTO

**On camera:** Heidi foreground right in this closer angle; Otto background left at the latch. Sadie and Penny are outside this framing and silent.

**00:24–00:25:** Heidi finishes turning her gaze toward Otto. He notices and freezes.

**00:25–00:28 — HEIDI, ON CAMERA:**
> “Otto. We’ve discussed this.”

Only Heidi speaks. Her mouth follows her words. Otto listens silently; no lip movement. Heidi addresses him without raising her voice.

**00:28–00:31:** Otto lowers his head slightly and noses the casement open again. Heidi relaxes against her bench. No reply from Otto.

**Sound bridge out:** Wind returns. During the final half-second, hear Bruno’s heavier footsteps and navy collar jingling from the hall. Neither visible dog produces those sounds.

**End state:** Window open and remains open for the rest of the scene. Heidi on her bench. Otto clear of the entrance route.

## 05 · Meet the grandson
### 00:31–00:38 · CUT TO DOORWAY, CHAIR, AND HEIDI

**On camera:** Bruno enters through the rear doorway, then moves to his chair at screen right. Heidi stays visible at left. Sadie beyond the table. Penny partially obscured beneath it. Otto remains near the window in the background.

**00:31–00:33:** Bruno enters on the footsteps heard before the cut. He looks toward Heidi.

**00:33–00:35 — BRUNO, ON CAMERA:**
> “Morning, Grandmother.”

Only Bruno speaks, in his warm male baritone. Heidi silently acknowledges him. Sadie watches without speaking.

**00:35–00:38:** Bruno wedges his forequarters onto the chair, leaving his hind paws on the floor. His large rump nudges Penny’s bowl beneath the chair. Hold long enough to understand his ridiculous posture. No human-style sitting.

**End state:** Bruno awkwardly perched; bowl displaced but not broken. Heidi looks directly at him. The camera holds this composition into the continuation.

## 06 · Grandmother’s authority
### 00:38–00:42 · CONTINUE 05

**On camera:** Same cast and positions. Heidi’s face and Bruno’s listening reaction both visible.

**00:38–00:40.5 — HEIDI, ON CAMERA:**
> “All four paws down, Bruno.”

Only Heidi speaks. Bruno’s mouth remains closed. The spoken name “Bruno” does not change speakers.

**00:40.5–00:42:** Bruno yields, shifting his weight backward and starting to lower his front paws from the chair. Heidi watches silently.

**End state:** Bruno is midway through getting down. Continue that motion without resetting his posture.

## 07 · Meet their daughter
### 00:42–00:50 · CONTINUE 06

**On camera:** Bruno, Penny, Sadie; Heidi remains at the left of the composition. Otto stays quietly in the background.

**00:42–00:43.5:** Bruno completes his descent onto all four paws. Penny emerges from under the table and looks up at him, then toward her bowl pinned beside his rear paw.

**00:43.5–00:47 — PENNY, ON CAMERA:**
> “Dad, you’re sitting on my breakfast.”

Only the small lavender-collared puppy speaks, in her established young female voice. Bruno listens with his mouth closed, then follows her gaze downward. No adult mouths Penny’s line.

**00:47–00:50:** Bruno shifts his paw with embarrassed care. Sadie starts moving toward the bowl from her established side of the table. Penny watches her mother.

**End state:** Sadie is approaching the bowl. Cut during that movement; the following shot picks up the same action.

## 08 · Meet the mother
### 00:50–00:58 · CUT ON SADIE’S APPROACH

**On camera:** Sadie and Penny in the foreground; Bruno’s face and navy collar visible at the frame edge. Heidi and Otto are outside this closer framing.

**00:50–00:52:** Match Sadie’s approach from the preceding shot. With her muzzle she slides the bowl clear of the chair and toward Penny. Finish the object manipulation before dialogue begins.

**00:52–00:56.5 — SADIE, ON CAMERA:**
> “Penny, your father thinks every chair belongs to him.”

Only Sadie speaks, in her precise adult female voice. Penny looks up silently before eating. Bruno listens silently. The addressed name “Penny” does not change speakers.

**00:56.5–00:58:** Sadie glances from Penny to Bruno. Her amused expression becomes more appraising. Penny starts eating from the floor bowl; the cheddar cube remains on its separate plate.

**End state:** Sadie turns toward Bruno. Match that turn into the two-shot.

## 09 · Husband and wife
### 00:58–01:06 · CUT TO SADIE AND BRUNO

**On camera:** Sadie at screen left, Bruno at screen right. Penny visible lower in the background with her bowl. Heidi and Otto offscreen and silent. Preserve these left/right positions throughout the exchange.

**00:58–00:59:** Complete Sadie’s turn. Bruno notices that he has her full attention.

**00:59–01:01 — SADIE, ON CAMERA:**
> “You came home late.”

Only Sadie speaks. Bruno listens, mouth closed.

**01:01–01:02:** A full silent beat. Sadie’s mouth closes before Bruno replies.

**01:02–01:04 — BRUNO, ON CAMERA:**
> “Sadie, I went for a walk.”

Only Bruno speaks. “Sadie” is the person he addresses, not a change to the female speaker. Sadie listens without moving her mouth.

**01:04–01:06:** Sadie studies him. Bruno attempts an innocent expression. Penny continues breakfast quietly behind them.

**End state:** Both adults remain face-to-face. No camera or position reset before the sniff.

## 10 · A suspicious aroma
### 01:06–01:15 · CONTINUE 09

**On camera:** Same two-shot, same positions; Penny remains in the background.

**01:06–01:09:** Sadie leans forward and sniffs Bruno’s muzzle. Both are silent. Bruno holds still.

**01:09–01:10:** Sadie withdraws a few inches. Recognition lands. Preserve this pause.

**01:10–01:13 — SADIE, ON CAMERA:**
> “A walk that serves Parmesan?”

Only Sadie speaks. Bruno does not answer. His mouth stays closed; his eyes shift slightly away. Penny pauses eating to watch.

**01:13–01:14.5:** Hold Bruno’s silent reaction. No invented denial or extra line.

**01:14.5 — HEIDI BEGINS OFFSCREEN:**
> “We can investigate…”

This is the beginning of the single line completed in segment 11. Heidi speaks from her bench outside this framing. **Sadie, Bruno, and Penny stay silent and do not mouth her words.** They begin turning toward her.

**Audio handoff:** Produce Heidi’s complete sentence once, as specified below, and carry it over the cut. Never stitch two separately performed halves or repeat “We can investigate.”

## 11 · Grandmother calls breakfast to order
### 01:15–01:23 · CUT ON HEIDI’S INTERRUPTION

**On camera:** Re-establish the same room master: Heidi beside the open window; Otto quietly nearby; Sadie and Bruno around the table; Penny near the cheese plate. No character changes seats or teleports across the room.

**HEIDI — ONE CONTINUOUS LINE, 01:14.5–01:19:**
> “We can investigate your marriage after breakfast.”

The line begins offscreen before the cut and finishes with Heidi visibly speaking. Her mouth follows the same continuous recording after the cut. Everyone else listens silently, looking toward her. Her tone is practical, not a theatrical shout.

**01:19–01:23:** Bruno and Sadie break their confrontation. Heidi settles back with satisfaction. Otto gives a small, silent nod. Penny glances from the adults to the cheddar cube at the near edge of the table.

**End state:** The cube is still on its plate. Penny is close enough to reach it with her mouth. Hold the same framing into segment 12.

## 12 · The real family priority
### 01:23–01:30 · CONTINUE 11

**On camera:** All five dogs in the established wide. Keep Penny and the cheese plate readable; no close-up insert is needed.

**01:23–01:25:** While the adults finish looking toward Heidi, Penny stretches her muzzle to the near edge of the low table and takes the single cube gently. No hands, utensil, or unexplained disappearance. The audience sees the theft.

**01:25–01:27:** Heidi looks toward the plate. Bruno and Sadie follow her gaze. Empty. Their eyes move to Penny, who finishes chewing and swallows.

**01:27–01:28 — PENNY, ON CAMERA:**
> “What?”

Only Penny speaks, after swallowing. The adults remain silent, mouths closed.

**01:28–01:30:** Hold the family’s reactions: Bruno faintly impressed, Sadie unsurprised, Heidi offended by the breach of etiquette, Otto quietly looking away. One light piano punctuation; continuous wind and breakfast-room tone underneath.

**End:** Hard cut at 01:30. No mystery reveal, teaser, additional character, or automatic fade.

## Acceptance before assembly

The production agent checks these points; they are not tasks for the viewer:

1. The exterior-to-interior opening is one connected camera move through the same open window. Otto enters through the doorway, not through the window or by teleportation.
2. The room, collars, character sizes, positions, and props remain recognizable through every cut. Bruno is the largest adult; Penny is unmistakably a puppy.
3. Every spoken line belongs to its named speaker. Offscreen Heidi never animates Otto’s, Sadie’s, or Bruno’s mouth. No extra dialogue or narrator appears.
4. Continuations contain only the newly scheduled action and dialogue. No preceding line or motion repeats at a generation boundary.
5. Heidi’s final interruption is one uninterrupted audio performance across its picture cut. Other cuts preserve room tone without introducing a visual dissolve.
6. Names and relationships are understandable from the performances: Heidi calls Otto; Bruno calls Heidi Grandmother; Heidi names Bruno; Penny calls him Dad; Sadie names Penny and identifies her father; Bruno names Sadie; Heidi identifies the marriage.
7. The cheese is present, visibly taken by Penny, then absent. The final reaction follows that visible cause.
8. The result runs 90 seconds and ends on the family joke. Audience comprehension takes priority over adding spectacle or advancing the mystery.
