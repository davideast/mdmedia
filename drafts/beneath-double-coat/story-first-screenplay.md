# Beneath the Double Coat
## Episode One: The Secret in the Snow

**Story cut — 2:10 planned.** A short adaptation of the original episode.

**Central question:** Who trapped Heidi, and what did she discover?

The audience starts with no knowledge of this family. The empty collar establishes loss; the fur offers hope; the locked room threatens that hope; Bruno’s reaction turns it into a family secret. The final lookalike gives that secret a visible shape.


### 1. A home and its ruler

**A house built for winter**

Dawn. A wide view establishes one snowbound Alpine mansion and a small toolshed beyond its garden. Warm windows; a single open ground-floor window. End on that open window.

*Sound: Wind outside; a restrained original piano motif.*

**Meet the mistress of the house**

Inside the open window is Heidi, an elderly Bernese resting on a marble floor. Otto, a stocky Bernese with a cleaning cloth, noses the window almost shut. Heidi lifts her head and gives him a commanding look. Frame both dogs and the window clearly.

> **HEIDI:** Otto. The window.
> **OTTO:** It is snowing, Mrs. von Alpenrose.

*Sound: Wind diminishes as the window closes. Distinct elderly female and earnest male voices.*

**Cold is comfort**

Continue the same view. Heidi noses the window open again, settles into the cold air and sighs contentedly. Otto quietly gives up. Let her satisfaction land.

> **HEIDI:** Yes. Lovely.

*Sound: Fresh wind; contented canine exhale. A tiny amused piano resolution.*


### 2. An unhappy marriage

**A husband on thin ice**

Breakfast room beside the hall. Sadie, in an emerald collar, waits across from Bruno in a navy collar. Bruno has his front paws on a chair and his rear paws on the floor. One cheese cube sits on a low breakfast table between them. Sadie looks at her husband, not the camera.

> **SADIE:** Bruno. You came home at two.
> **BRUNO:** I went for a walk.

*Sound: Quiet breakfast room. Precise female voice; smooth evasive male voice.*

**The peace offering fails**

Continue the breakfast two-shot. Bruno nudges the cheese cube toward Sadie with his nose. She ignores the offering and leans toward his muzzle. He goes still.

> **SADIE:** For six hours?
> **BRUNO:** Sadie—

*Sound: Cheese plate scrapes gently; let the uncomfortable pause play.*

**A very specific suspicion**

Continue. Sadie sniffs Bruno’s muzzle, then withdraws slowly. Bruno avoids her gaze. Neither eats the cheese. Hold his guilty reaction.

> **SADIE:** My husband smells like another dog’s Parmesan.

*Sound: Music drops away for the accusation. No comic sound effect.*


### 3. A place kept for someone missing

**The place kept empty**

Hallway outside breakfast. Heidi stops at a low console holding a photograph of two nearly identical Bernese puppies and an empty faded brown collar. Bruno enters from the breakfast doorway behind her. Camera stays close enough to read the two puppies as a pair without any written labels.

> **BRUNO:** Grandmother. You cannot keep waiting for him.

*Sound: The opening piano motif returns, quieter. Bruno is gentle but tense.*

**Heidi refuses to let go**

Continue the same hallway shot. Heidi noses the empty collar back beside the photograph. Bruno looks away and returns toward breakfast. She watches him leave, then lowers her nose toward the collar.

> **HEIDI:** Then stop telling me to put his things away.

*Sound: Soft collar buckle on wood; a brief silence after the line.*


### 4. Hope follows a physical clue

**Something has come inside**

Continue with Heidi. Her nose pauses. Camera tilts to fresh muddy paw prints crossing the clean hall from the outside door toward the grooming room. Heidi notices and follows. Show the trail and destination together.

*Sound: A sniff, slow paw steps. A low suspense note replaces the family motif.*

**The familiar scent**

Grooming room, reached from the same hall. Heidi follows the muddy prints to a low grooming platform. A silver rake and a tuft of black fur lie on it. In one legible view she smells the fur, stills, then turns her head toward the hallway she came from.

*Sound: Ventilation hum is clearly audible. Sniffing stops suddenly.*

**Hope before danger**

Continue from Heidi’s reaction. She lifts her ears and takes one hopeful step toward the open doorway, expecting someone. Keep the fur visible behind her. There is nobody in the doorway.

> **HEIDI:** Sebastian? You are alive?

*Sound: Vent continues. Her voice softens with hope; no ominous sting yet.*


### 5. Discovery causes danger

**Someone is listening**

Continue. A canine shadow passes across the hallway outside. Heidi turns toward it. The door closes before she reaches it. Never reveal the dog casting the shadow.

> **HEIDI:** Who is there?

*Sound: A paw step outside, then a clear latch click.*

**The room changes**

Continue with Heidi pushing at the same door; it does not open. She stops when the vent above her becomes silent, then looks from the vent toward the nearby thermostat. Show this cause-and-reaction sequence without leaving the room.

*Sound: Door rattles under her paw. Ventilation hum cuts out sharply. Hold the new stillness.*

**An outrage, not a medical emergency**

Continue. Camera follows her gaze toward the thermostat, then returns to her offended expression. The room is only mildly warmer: no collapse or injury. A readable editorial thermostat insert will show 72°F becoming 74°F; do not rely on model lettering to explain the action.

> **HEIDI:** You would not.

*Sound: A thermostat beep. Small rising strings below her voice.*

**The alarm**

Continue. Heidi plants her front paws against the locked door and scratches furiously, rear paws firmly on the floor. Her bark-like final shout carries into the hall. End with the door still closed.

> **HEIDI:** YOU MONSTER!

*Sound: Loud scratching, her theatrical shout, then a short musical sting.*


### 6. The family converges

**Someone hears**

The hallway outside the same grooming door. Otto, already introduced at the window, stops cleaning when he hears Heidi. He follows the scratching to the door and noses down its large lever handle.

> **OTTO:** Mrs. von Alpenrose?

*Sound: Scratching is muffled through the door; Otto’s concerned voice.*

**An accusation interrupted**

Return to the established breakfast two-shot. Heidi’s distant shout has interrupted Sadie. Bruno turns toward the hall and steps away from the untouched cheese; Sadie follows him.

> **SADIE:** We are not finished.
> **BRUNO:** That was Grandmother.

*Sound: Heidi’s shout carries faintly from the hall before their dialogue; hurried paws follow.*

**What she rescued**

Grooming doorway, now open. Otto stands to one side as Heidi emerges carrying the tuft of fur gently in her mouth. Bruno and Sadie arrive from the hall. She is indignant, not injured. She ignores the question and places the fur at Bruno’s front paws.

> **BRUNO:** Grandmother. Are you hurt?

*Sound: Paw steps stop; breathing settles. No music over Bruno’s question.*


### 7. The secret has a name

**Bruno recognizes it too**

Continue in the same doorway group. Heidi indicates the fur with her nose. Bruno smells it; his confident posture collapses into stillness. Sadie notices his reaction and looks from him to Heidi.

> **HEIDI:** Someone locked me in. After I found this.

*Sound: A single sniff, then an exposed pause. Sparse low piano.*

**A family secret becomes public**

Continue. Heidi watches Bruno as she says the name. Sadie turns toward her husband. Bruno answers her directly after a reluctant pause. Keep all three in the same frame so the relationships are unmistakable.

> **HEIDI:** Sebastian’s.
> **SADIE:** Who is Sebastian?
> **BRUNO:** My brother.

*Sound: Music stays low. Three distinct voices with space between turns.*

**The story he told**

Continue. Heidi holds Bruno’s gaze. Sadie slowly turns back to Bruno. He has no answer. Hold that unanswered look for two seconds.

> **HEIDI:** The one you said was dead.

*Sound: Let the line land in silence, followed by one low note.*


### 8. A visual cliffhanger

**Someone watching the house**

Exterior garden at the same morning light. Fresh paw prints lead to the toolshed established in the first shot. A large Bernese in a faded brown collar stands in its doorway, watching the mansion. Push close enough to reveal the same facial markings as the household Bruno. Match their approved face reference; this is not an unrelated dog.

*Sound: Wind and a faint collar jingle. A rising suspense chord.*

**Two places at once**

Hard cut back to household Bruno in the navy collar, still with Heidi and Sadie in the established grooming doorway. Match his face size and angle to the previous shot. Sadie waits for his answer. No dog moves between locations.

*Sound: Hold the suspense chord, then stop it.*

**The question that remains**

Editorial black title card: BENEATH THE DOUBLE COAT. Smaller line: The Secret in the Snow. Exact typography is added in editing, not generated inside a moving scene.

*Sound: One mournful original piano note.*
