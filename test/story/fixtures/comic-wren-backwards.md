## Page 2

layout: 1 | 1 1 | 1 1
rules: >
  Wren and Gilbert are in the mirror world: everything on their side is silvery,
  moonlit and backwards, like an old silver photograph, while their own colors
  are faded and pale.

### Panel 1

> Wide shot of the silver mirror-world landing. Wren sits sprawled on the
> floor, dazed; a clock on the wall, the rain outside the window falling
> upward. On the stairs sits Gilbert. Left: WREN. Right: GILBERT. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Wren is bewildered; Gilbert is shy and hopeful.

*(wondering)* Everything was silver. And outside, the rain was falling up.

**GILBERT** *(soft)* [small]: Psst.

### Panel 2

> Medium shot. Wren hugs Gilbert tight, kneeling on the silver stairs. Center:
> WREN and GILBERT. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Wren is overjoyed and teary; Gilbert's pinned ear squashed against
> her cheek.

**WREN** *(whispering, amazed)*: Gilbert?

**GILBERT** *(tender)*: I'm his reflection. Hello, Wren.

### Panel 3

> Through the shop window, seen from the silver side: on the real street, in
> full color, Nerw sweeps the shop step while Pim stands in the fish shop
> doorway with a bucket. Left: NERW. Right: PIM. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Nerw smiles politely and blankly; Pim looks at her, puzzled.

**PIM** *(cheeky)*: He kept losing his plaice.

**NERW** *(polite)*: That's very clever, Pim.

### Panel 4

> Wren and Gilbert sit behind the glass on the silver side, watching. Left:
> WREN. Right: GILBERT. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Wren is small and sad, knees hugged to her chest; Gilbert worried,
> his ear drooping.

**WREN** *(small, sad)*: Maybe I should just stay. They like her more.

**GILBERT** *(worried, grave)*: Reflections who swap never give it back.

### Panel 5

> In the real shop, in color, Nerw throws a sheet over a moon-shaped mirror,
> more sheets over her arm; covered mirrors stand all around. Center: NERW.
> Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Nerw hums, pleased and tidy, completely calm.

*(ominous)* One by one, the mirrors went dark.
