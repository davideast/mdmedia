# Midnight Line

### The Midnight Line

```music
Melancholic piano and solitary cello with a slow rhythm.
```

*(numb, crushed)* I lost by one hundredth of a second.

*(bitter, obsessed)* That's not a number. That's a blink. That's the time it takes a hummingbird to think about flapping. And it was the whole difference between me, Akari Mori, regional champion, and me, Akari Mori, second place.

*(envious, quiet)* Sora Hayashi won. Of course Sora Hayashi won. Sora with his perfect silver hair and his perfect calm face, standing on the top step with the gold medal, not even smiling.

*(haunted, fixated)* I didn't take the train home with the team. I sat on the bleachers until the lights went off, replaying the start in my head. Bang. Go. Bang. Go. Where was the hundredth? Where did I lose it?

*(exhausted)* By the time I got to Kitaguchi Station, the last train was gone.

**AKARI** *(sarcastic, defeated)*: Great. Perfect. Amazing day.

*(quiet, watchful)* The station clock said 11:59.

*(tense, uneasy)* Then it said 12:00, and the lights turned blue.

*(awestruck, eerie)* A train came out of the tunnel without making a sound. It was old, older than my grandma, with round windows and brass handles, and paper lanterns swinging in every carriage. The sign on the front didn't say a station. It just said MIDNIGHT LINE.

*(hesitant)* The doors slid open in front of me.

**TAMA** *(sharp, impatient)*: Well? Are you getting on or not? Some of us have a schedule.

*(surprised, bewildered)* A cat was sitting in the doorway. A round calico cat, wearing a tiny conductor's cap, with a ticket punch on a cord around her neck.

*(stunned)* She had two tails.

**AKARI** *(speechless)*: You can talk.

**TAMA** *(sarcastic, unimpressed)*: And you can stand there with your mouth open. We all have gifts.

*(deadpan)* She flicked both tails.

**TAMA** *(curt, commanding)*: I'm Tama. Get on.

*(puzzled, resigned)* I don't know why I got on. Maybe because it was that or walk home. Maybe because a cat with two tails had just told me to, and when that happens you kind of have to find out what's next.

*(trapped)* The doors closed behind me. The train began to move.

*(unsettled, observing)* The carriage was full. A salaryman stared at nothing. A girl in a kimono stared at nothing. Everyone sat very still, very calm, holding glowing paper tickets in their laps.

**TAMA** *(businesslike)*: Fare, please.

**AKARI** *(confused, hesitant)*: I don't have a ticket.

**TAMA** *(matter-of-fact)*: Everybody has a ticket.

*(direct)* She tapped my chest with one paw.

**TAMA** *(smooth, alluring)*: Right there. The moment you'd most like to forget. You hand it over, I punch it, and you never have to feel it again. That's the fare.

*(startled, mesmerized)* Something warm and bright pulled at my chest. When I looked down, there was a ticket made of light, and on it, in tiny numbers, it said 0.01.

*(realizing, pained)* The hundredth.

*(tempted, vulnerable)* For one second I wanted to give it to her. I really did.

**AKARI** *(sudden, resolute)*: No.

*(wary)* Tama's ears went flat.

**TAMA** *(dangerous, sharp)*: No?

**AKARI** *(fierce, stubborn)*: It's mine. I lost it. I'm keeping it.

*(chilling, ominous)* The lanterns flickered. Every passenger in the carriage turned to look at me at the same time.

**TAMA** *(hushed, delighted)*: Oh, you've done it now.

*(knowing)* And I swear she sounded pleased.

*(alarmed, sudden)* The connecting door banged open, and in poured crows. Huge black crows in little conductor caps, with brass buttons on their chests and ticket punches in their beaks.

**CROW** *(shrieking, aggressive)*: STOWAWAY! STOWAWAY! FARE! FARE!

*(instinctive)* I ran.

*(desperate, breathless)* I'm good at running. It's the one thing I'm good at. I ran through the next carriage and the crows came after me, a storm of feathers and clacking beaks, and the lead crow dived straight at my face.

*(stunned, quiet)* And the world slowed down.

*(surreal, frozen)* The crow hung in the air in front of me, wings spread, beak open. A lantern froze mid-swing. A bead of light drifted off my ticket like a firefly.

*(disbelieving, breathless)* I could move. Nobody else could.

*(swift, frantic)* I stepped around the crow. I ducked under its wing. I ran to the end of the carriage, and the world snapped back to speed, and the crow smacked beak-first into the seat where I'd been.

**AKARI** *(panting, astonished)*: What was that?

**TAMA** *(cool, explanatory)*: That was a hundredth of a second. Yours. You didn't sell it, so the line turned it into something else.

*(observant)* She looked almost proud.

**TAMA** *(grudgingly impressed)*: One Hundredth. Not bad, stowaway.

### Passengers of Light

```music
Mysterious ambient synths and gentle woodwinds creating a subtle tension.
```

*(relieved, anxious)* In the next carriage, I found Sora.

*(observant, worried)* He was sitting by the window with his gold medal around his neck, looking out at the dark.

**AKARI** *(desperate, breathless)*: Sora!

*(tense)* I grabbed his shoulder.

**AKARI** *(urgent, bewildered)*: What are you doing here?

*(unsettled, unnerved)* He turned and looked at me politely. Just politely. Like I was a stranger asking for directions.

**SORA** *(dazed, politely detached)*: I think I missed my train.

**AKARI** *(incredulous, frantic)*: You're on a ghost train with talking cats and crows!

**SORA** *(mildly curious, flat)*: Am I?

*(disturbed)* He looked down at his medal like he wasn't sure whose it was.

**SORA** *(distant, faintly amused)*: I suppose that's interesting.

*(shaken, bitter)* Sora Hayashi had never once in his life said anything was interesting.

*(shaken, bitter)* He said things were fine, or not fine.

*(shaken, bitter)* He trained harder than anybody. He wanted it more than anybody. That was the worst thing about him.

*(haunted, heartbroken)* And now he didn't want anything at all.

```pause
1.5s
```

**STATIONMASTER** *(gentle, eerily smooth)*: Ah. Another passenger who hasn't paid.

*(intimidated, cautious)* The man in the doorway was so tall he had to bow his head under the lanterns. He wore a long navy coat with brass buttons and white gloves, and his cap was pulled low so I couldn't see his eyes. In one hand he held a pocket watch with no hands. It glowed.

**STATIONMASTER** *(soothing, softly polite)*: I'm the Stationmaster. And I'm so sorry about your race.

**AKARI** *(suspicious, defensive)*: How do you know about my race?

**STATIONMASTER** *(calm, insidious)*: I know about everyone's worst moment. That's my job.

*(wary)* He opened the watch. Inside, a hundred glowing tickets turned slowly, like fish in a pond.

**STATIONMASTER** *(persuasive, quiet)*: I take them away. That's all. Your friend gave me his, and look how peaceful he is.

**AKARI** *(sharp, quick to deny)*: He's not my friend.

**STATIONMASTER** *(smoothly dismissive)*: Then you won't mind.

*(unsettled)* He held out his gloved hand.

**STATIONMASTER** *(tempting, velvety)*: Give me the hundredth, Akari. You'll never feel it again. Not tonight. Not ever. No more lying awake. No more bang, go, bang, go.

*(chilled, shaken)* How did he know about bang, go?

*(vulnerable, tempted)* My ticket glowed in my chest, warm and heavy. I thought about not feeling it. Not lying awake. Not replaying the start for the rest of my life.

**AKARI** *(hesitant, guarding herself)*: I'll think about it.

**STATIONMASTER** *(understanding, pleasantly sinister)*: Of course.

*(watchful)* He smiled under his cap.

**STATIONMASTER** *(courteous, dark)*: The doors seal at dawn. Take your time.

*(startled)* When he was gone, someone tugged my sleeve.

*(astonished, taking it in)* A tiny old woman was sitting on the bench behind me, knitting. She had round glasses and a purple shawl, and six thin arms, all knitting at once. A silver scarf ran from her needles out the door and down the whole length of the train.

**FUJI** *(warm, crackly, approving)*: You've got manners. You didn't shout at me. Most people shout at me.

**AKARI** *(baffled, polite)*: Why would I shout at you?

**FUJI** *(matter-of-fact, amused)*: Six arms, dear.

*(softly)* She smiled.

**FUJI** *(gently intriguing)*: I'm Grandma Fuji. Would you like to know what your friend gave him?

*(focused, amazed)* She held up the end of her scarf. Silver threads glowed, and in them I saw a picture: Sora, last year, at the start line. He moved before the gun. Just a twitch. Nobody saw it. Nobody but him.

*(understanding, quiet revelation)* He won that race. And he'd been training like a maniac ever since, trying to win one fair and square.

**FUJI** *(soft, solemn)*: He gave that away. And with it, the reason he runs. That's the trick, dear. Everything you'd like to forget is tied to something you love. The Stationmaster takes both.

**AKARI** *(dreading, quiet)*: And at dawn?

**FUJI** *(sober, somber)*: At dawn the doors seal, and everyone aboard rides forever. Calm and quiet and empty. And the Stationmaster grows strong enough to be a god again.

*(grim, realizing the urgency)* I looked out the window. The sky over the city was turning grey.

**AKARI** *(resolute, bracing herself)*: Okay. Okay. How do I get Sora's ticket back?

*(listening)* Grandma Fuji's needles clicked.

**FUJI** *(simple, direct)*: It's in the watch.

**AKARI** *(flat, processing the absurdity)*: The watch the giant god is holding.

**FUJI** *(cheerfully unbothered)*: Mm-hm.

*(surprised)* Tama sat down next to my feet.

**TAMA** *(dry, sharp, pragmatic)*: If you're going to do something stupid, do it on the roof. More room.

### Roof Chase

```music
Fast-paced rhythmic percussion and intense strings building urgency.
```

*(bracing against the gale)* I climbed out the window. The wind hit me like a wall.

*(overwhelmed, determined)* The roof of the Midnight Line stretched ahead of me, carriage after carriage, rushing over the rooftops of the city, and at the very front of the train, the Stationmaster stood with his coat whipping in the wind and his watch glowing in his hand.

*(alarmed)* Behind me, the crows came screaming out of the windows.

**CROW** *(furious, screeching)*: FARE! FARE! STOWAWAY!

*(panicked)* I ran.

*(focused, strained)* One hundredth. The crows froze in the air. I jumped the gap between carriages.

*(tense, breathless)* One hundredth. A crow's beak, an inch from my ear, holding still. I ducked.

*(exhausted, in pain)* One hundredth. One hundredth. One hundredth. Each one felt like a long, slow heartbeat, and every one hurt a little, like a stitch in my side.

*(desperate, reckless)* The gap before the front carriage was too wide. I jumped anyway.

*(terrified)* I wasn't going to make it.

*(gasping, relieved)* A silver thread whipped out of the window below me and caught my wrist. Grandma Fuji's scarf swung me up and over, and I landed on the front carriage, right in front of the Stationmaster.

**STATIONMASTER** *(gentle, eerily soft)*: Akari, you don't have to hurt like this.

**AKARI** *(pained)*: I know.

*(quietly)* I said.

**AKARI** *(fierce)*: I want to.

*(strained, resolute)* I used my last hundredth. The world went still. The Stationmaster's hand hung in the air, and in it the watch, open, full of light.

*(awe-struck, desperate)* I reached in and found Sora's ticket. It was warm.

*(jarred)* The world snapped back.

**STATIONMASTER** *(booming, enraged)*: No!

*(desperate)* The Stationmaster's shadow spread across the roof, huge and dark. I threw the ticket down through the open window.

**AKARI** *(fierce, shouting)*: SORA! CATCH!

*(holding her breath)* For one second nothing happened.

*(amazed)* Then the train shook. Wind roared up through the carriage, out of the windows, a gust so strong it blew the crows away like leaves. And Sora came up out of that wind, riding it like a wave, his silver hair everywhere and his eyes wide awake.

**SORA** *(astonished, strained)*: Mori, what did you do?

**AKARI** *(exultant, breathless)*: Gave you back your problem. You're welcome!

*(urgent)* The sun came up over the edge of the city. The doors began to close.

*(breathless)* Sora grabbed my hand.

**SORA** *(firm, urgent)*: Hold on.

*(exhilarated, warm)* His wind picked us both up and blew us off the roof of the Midnight Line, out into the morning air, and the last thing I saw was Tama in the window, winking at me with both tails held high.

**AKARI** *(gentle, steady)*: I know.

**SORA** *(vulnerable, soft)*: I've been trying to make up for it ever since.

**AKARI** *(warm, quiet)*: I know that too.

*(gentle)* I sat down next to him.

**AKARI** *(lightly teasing)*: You still beat me by a hundredth.

**SORA** *(softly)*: I know.

*(surprised, soft)* Then, for the first time ever, Sora Hayashi smiled.

**SORA** *(a quiet smile in his voice, hopeful)*: Rematch?

*(looking ahead, resolute)* I looked down the tracks to the next station.

**AKARI** *(determined, sharp)*: Ready. Bang.

*(exhilarated, surging forward)* We ran.
