## Page 1

layout: 1 | 1 1 | 1 1

### Panel 1

> Wide shot of an empty, old Japanese train platform at midnight, lit an eerie
> blue. Akari sits alone on a bench with her sports bag, scowling, as a silent
> old train with round windows and glowing paper lanterns slides out of the
> tunnel. Left: AKARI. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Akari is sulking hard after losing; she hasn't noticed the train is
> strange yet.

*(flat, still stinging)* I lost by one hundredth of a second.

**AKARI** *(sarcastic, to nobody)*: Great. Perfect. Amazing day.

### Panel 2

> Medium shot at the open train door. Akari stands on the platform; in the
> doorway sits Tama, small, looking up at her. Left: AKARI. Right: TAMA.
> Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Akari stares, mouth open; Tama is bored and impatient, as if this
> happens every night.

**TAMA** *(impatient, sardonic)*: Well? Are you getting on or not? Some of us have a schedule.

### Panel 3

> Close-up of Tama sitting in the doorway, both tails flicking. Center: TAMA.
> Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Smug and unimpressed, one eyebrow up.

**AKARI** *(stunned)*: You can talk.

**TAMA** *(dry)*: And you can stand there with your mouth open. We all have gifts.

### Panel 4

> Inside the carriage. Still, blank-faced passengers hold small glowing paper
> tickets in their laps. Tama, standing on a seat, taps Akari's chest with one
> paw, and a small ticket made of light glows there. Left: AKARI. Right: TAMA.
> Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Akari looks down at the glowing ticket, torn; then her jaw sets in
> refusal. Tama's ears go flat.

**TAMA** *(businesslike)*: The moment you'd most like to forget. That's the fare.

**AKARI** *(stubborn, quiet)*: No.

### Panel 5

> The connecting door bursts open and a storm of crows in tiny conductor caps
> pours through, beaks open, wings everywhere. Center: CROW. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Furious, shrieking, swarming straight at the reader.

**CROW** *(shrieking)* [loud]: STOWAWAY! FARE! FARE!

## Page 2

layout: 1 | 1 1 | 1 1

### Panel 1

> Wide action shot down the carriage, frozen in time: a crow hangs mid-dive in
> the air, a paper lantern frozen mid-swing, sparks of light hanging still.
> Akari steps around the frozen crow, trailing glowing blue afterimages behind
> her, with speed lines. Left: AKARI. Right: CROW. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Akari is amazed and moving fast; the crow is completely frozen.

*(awed)* And the world slowed down.

**TAMA** *(almost proud)*: One Hundredth. Not bad, stowaway.

### Panel 2

> Medium shot. Sora sits by the train window with his gold medal; Akari grabs
> his shoulder from the aisle. Left: AKARI. Right: SORA. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Akari is urgent; Sora looks up at her politely and emptily, as if
> she were a stranger.

**AKARI** *(urgent)*: Sora! What are you doing here?

**SORA** *(polite, distant)*: I think I missed my train.

### Panel 3

> Low angle. The Stationmaster stands in the doorway, so tall he bows his head
> under the lanterns, his cap shadowing his eyes. He holds his pocket watch
> open, and inside it glowing tickets turn slowly like fish in a pond. Center:
> STATIONMASTER. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Gentle, kind and terribly polite, holding out one gloved hand; the
> kindness is unsettling.

**STATIONMASTER** *(gentle, soothing)*: Give me the hundredth, Akari. You'll never feel it again.

### Panel 4

> Close-up of Akari, her small glowing ticket bright against her chest.
> Center: AKARI. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Truly tempted, eyes shining, biting her lip.

**AKARI** *(unsure, quiet)* [small]: I'll think about it.

### Panel 5

> Grandma Fuji sits on a bench knitting with all six arms; the silver scarf
> glows, and in its threads is a picture of Sora twitching at a running start
> line. Akari leans in to look. Left: FUJI. Right: AKARI. Balloons: top.
>
> acting: Fuji is warm and grandmotherly; Akari's face falls as she
> understands.

**FUJI** *(gentle, knowing)*: Everything you'd like to forget is tied to something you love. He takes both.
