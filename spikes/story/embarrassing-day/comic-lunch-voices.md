# The Most Embarrassing Day of My Life — Lunch

**MILO** *(warm, smug, building up the good news)*: By lunch, things were going better than I'd imagined.

**HARPER** *(loud and delighted, calling across the cafeteria)*: Milo! Dude!

**MILO** *(giddy, can't believe it)*: An actual high five.

**MILO** *(even more giddy, savoring the name)*: From Harper Blake.

**MILO** *(proud, a little smug)*: At our lunch table.

**HARPER** *(warm, flattering)*: You're definitely one of my favorites.

**HARPER** *(gushing, excited)*: That clip? Hilarious. We're using it in the assembly.

**MILO** *(the fun drains out, sudden dread)*: My smile froze.

**MILO** *(uneasy, trying to sound casual)*: Wait. Using it?

**HARPER** *(breezy, already leaving)*: Yeah! Right before we announce the new host. It's going to kill!

**JUNE** *(flat, quietly suspicious)*: What clip?

**MILO** *(guilty, avoiding)*: I stared at my tray.

**JUNE** *(firmer, waiting for an answer)*: Milo?

**MILO** *(embarrassed, self-mocking)*: I tried to laugh. It sounded like a lawn mower failing to start.

**MILO** *(weak, nervous laugh)*: Heh. Okay. Funny story.

**JUNE** *(cold, slow, each word its own sentence)*: What. Clip.

**MILO** *(barely audible, ashamed)*: The dancing one.

**MILO** *(hopeful, then unsure)*: For one second, I thought she'd laugh.

**MILO** *(quiet, careful)*: Not angry.

**MILO** *(heavy, quiet)*: Worse. Hurt.

**JUNE** *(stunned, disbelieving)*: You sent my video?

**MILO** *(defensive, too fast)*: It was just to Harper.

**JUNE** *(hurt, quiet)*: You promised.

**MILO** *(pleading)*: I know, but—

**JUNE** *(hurt, voice catching)*: You promised, Milo.

**MILO** *(numb, distant)*: Around us, the cafeteria continued its usual chaos.

**KID** *(loud, indignant, far away)*: Ketchup is TOO a vegetable!

**MILO** *(hushed, heavy)*: But at our table, everything had gone quiet.

**MILO** *(ashamed, small)*: I thought it might help me win.

**MILO** *(pleading, desperate)*: June, come on.

**JUNE** *(quiet, steady, hurt)*: You know why I sent you that video?

**MILO** *(unsure, guessing)*: To see if the dance was good.

**JUNE** *(soft, sad)*: No.

**JUNE** *(voice shaking, holding back tears)*: Because I was scared. And you always make scary things feel less scary.

**JUNE** *(barely holding it together, almost a whisper)*: I guess that was stupid.

**MILO** *(hollow, alone)*: I sat there holding a french fry.

**MILO** *(rueful, sad but wry)*: It was probably the worst french fry of my entire life.
