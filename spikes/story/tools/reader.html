The Most Embarrassing Day of My Life

A middle school story about friendship, popularity, and one spectacularly bad decision. Press play and the panels follow the voices.

How this was made

Prototype build from one markdown story file. Voices: Gemini TTS in two-speaker runs, Milo narrating in his own voice. Music: one Lyria clip per scene, ducked under speech. Art: each version draws a character sheet first, then every panel against it.
